Privacy notice — UniZodiac Backup

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · Operator: nexplory

This notice covers the private UniZodiac Backup utility and its Google Drive access. It does not cover customer services of the public UniZodiac website.

Data access and use

With the account owner's consent, the utility uses the Google Drive API to upload, list and verify its backup files and related metadata, such as names, sizes and identifiers. It requests drive.file access to app-created or explicitly app-authorized files. It does not request access to unrelated Drive documents, Gmail, Contacts or Calendar.

The backup payload consists of the owner's development website content and associated data and settings needed for recovery. It is used only for backup verification, administration and recovery.

Storage and protection

Backup payloads are encrypted before upload to the owner's Google Drive account. Ordinary file metadata is not concealed by this encryption. Transfers use encrypted connections. Authorization credentials are access-restricted, and recovery keys are kept separately under the owner's control.

Sharing and limited use

The workflow does not publicly share backups. Google processes stored files as the storage provider under its applicable terms. Access is limited to the owner and authorized administrators. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to AI providers for model training.

UniZodiac Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Cloud backups remain until the owner deletes them. The owner can delete backup files directly in Google Drive and permanently remove them from Trash as needed. Revoking access through Google Account connections stops future authorized access but does not delete existing files. Stored authorization credentials can be removed separately by the owner or an authorized administrator.

Support and updates

Authorized users can use the support contact shown on the Google authorization consent screen for questions about this utility or its data handling. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this notice before the changed practice is used.

This information site

These static pages contain no analytics, advertising, sign-in forms or tracking scripts. When published on GitHub Pages, hosting-related connection information is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement.
