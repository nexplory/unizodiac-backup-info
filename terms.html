Use information — UniZodiac Backup

Effective date: October 17, 2026

UniZodiac Backup is operated by nexplory for private development administration. Only the owner and expressly authorized administrators may configure or use it. It is not a public or paid backup service.

Authorized use

Use requires the Google Drive account owner's authorization. Administrators must use it only for the authorized backup and recovery purpose, protect access credentials, and follow the privacy notice.

Availability and recovery

Backup success depends on valid authorization, available storage and successful execution. Scheduling alone does not guarantee recovery; administrators must verify backup results and retain the necessary recovery material.

Ending access and support

The owner may stop backups and revoke Google access at any time. Existing backups must be deleted separately if no longer required. Authorized users can use the support contact shown on the Google authorization consent screen.
